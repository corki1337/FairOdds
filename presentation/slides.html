Aichain

Finance without the bookmaker — pari-mutuel pools on Solana

Superteam Poland Challenge

Problem

Classic betting needs a trusted intermediary who holds funds, sets odds, and decides payouts.

That intermediary can delay, censor, or fail — and always takes a margin.

Insight

Write the terms into a Solana program. Custody in a PDA vault. Payouts are math, not discretion.

Product

  • Binary markets (NaVi vs FaZe)
  • demo-USDC on devnet
  • Mutual pool odds that update with every bet
  • Target user: esports fans

Payouts

payout = floor(stake × total_pool / winning_pool)

No vig from winners. Platform earns yield on idle capital via CPI to yield_park.

Architecture

  • aichain — markets, bets, resolve, claim
  • yield_park — deposit/withdraw + APR shares
  • OracleFeed PDA fixed at market init
  • HTTP source for Switchboard: /api/oracle/[id]

Live demo

  1. Place bets (2 wallets)
  2. Lock → CPI into yield_park
  3. Oracle finalize → Resolve
  4. Claim / Refund — show Solscan
