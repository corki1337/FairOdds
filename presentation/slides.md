# FairOdds - English deck

## 1. Your side. Your stake.

Transparent sports pools on Solana devnet.

A wallet signs the bet. Programs hold the rules and custody.

## 2. Players need verifiable rules.

Opaque pools, custody and discretionary resolution make traditional betting difficult to verify.

FairOdds exposes pool balances, deadlines, immutable feed addresses and receipts on-chain.

## 3. 0.5% of positive profit.

100 demo-USDC stake + 400 gross payout = 1.50 fee + 398.50 net.

No protocol fee on refunds. Existing policy-free markets retain 0%. Pool odds change until cutoff.

## 4. The full principal goes to winners.

Champion and top-scorer pools support 2-32 participants. Every deposit parks immediately.

0% protocol fee. Funded demo reserve surplus goes to treasury. No deployed Kamino or guaranteed APY.

## 5. Two feeds. One result.

Season providers must agree on a mapped participant after the league is final.

Pinned addresses and hashes, locked feed authority, timestamp checks. Missing data eventually enables refunds.

## 6. One interface. Any screen.

English / Polish, mobile layouts, live pools and clear kickoff times.

Claim controls distinguish winners, losers, refunds and already received funds. Empty sides never show fake odds.

## 7. Built for people and bots.

Solana Actions / Blinks + public REST + agent tools.

The API prepares unsigned transactions. Wallets and starter bots verify and sign locally.

## 8. Ready for owner deployment.

Local SBF integration tests, exact-money API tests and production build.

Upgrade both programs, configure provider keys / Redis / keeper, then record a real wallet run. Mainnet and production lending require separate review.
